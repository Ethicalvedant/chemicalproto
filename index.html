Our Story & Philosophy

Handmade With Passion • Rooted in Natural Science

RoseBloom began as an exploratory chemical prototyping project in our university laboratory. Inspired by the timeless beauty of freshly blossomed roses, we set out to investigate whether an elegant, nature-inspired fragrance could be created using accessible botanical extraction techniques without industrial synthetic fixatives.

Our Mission Statement

“Bringing the beauty of roses into an elegant, nature-inspired fragrance.”
01
Fresh Floral Inspiration

Observing morning roses, we captured the authentic dewy aroma that synthetic blends often miss.

02
Simple Formulation

Focusing on 4 high-purity components: petals, distilled water, cosmetic ethanol, and glycerin.

03
Sustainable Design

Reusable glass bottles and unbleached kraft paper packaging to minimize ecological footprint.

04
Student Accessible

Proving that artisan perfumery can be educational, transparent, and realistically priced.

💡
Ethical Academic Prototype Notice:

RoseBloom is presented truthfully as an experimental handmade prototype. In adherence to honest scientific communication, we make no unsubstantiated claims such as "100% chemical-free" (ethanol is a chemical solvent), "hypoallergenic", or "guaranteed all-day longevity". We invite testers to appreciate it as a gentle, intimate skin fragrance developed with genuine botanical care.

Storage & Preservation Instructions

Pure Formulation

The Four Essential Ingredients

No undisclosed binders or synthetic fixatives. Every raw material in RoseBloom was selected for a precise olfactory or chemical function.

Fragrance Core
Ingredient 01

Fresh Rose Petals

Hand-picked fresh rose petals serve as the primary source of the natural rose aroma. Their volatile aromatic oils give RoseBloom its characteristic delicate and romantic floral signature.

Extraction Method Gentle Maceration
Hydrosol Base
Ingredient 02

Distilled Water

Deionized, steam-distilled water free from mineral salts and contaminants. Used during the extraction process to produce a pure rose hydrosol that gently softens the fragrance.

Purity Grade Steam Distilled
Solvent & Base
Ingredient 03

Cosmetic Ethanol

High-purity cosmetic-grade ethanol acts as the universal perfume solvent. It dissolves floral terpenes, delivers a fine mist upon atomization, and naturally preserves the blend.

Function Carrier & Preservative
Natural Fixative
Ingredient 04

Vegetable Glycerin

Plant-derived humectant included as a supporting ingredient. It acts as a natural fixative to anchor volatile floral notes to the skin, providing smooth hydration and gentle projection.

Origin 100% Plant Sourced

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent information regarding our prototype formulation and care.

RoseBloom is an independently formulated product developed as part of a chemistry prototyping and small business demonstration. To maintain ethical consumer standards, we transparently describe it as a prototype until full third-party clinical certification and commercial mass-production testing are completed.

Because we use natural rose extracts rather than synthetic phthalate fixatives, RoseBloom has a natural wear time of approximately 3 to 5 hours. The vegetable glycerin in our formula helps anchor the fragrance molecules for a soft, comforting skin scent that evolves gracefully.

Cosmetic-grade ethanol is the standard organic solvent in traditional perfumery. It dissolves the hydrophobic essential oils extracted from the petals, enables a fine, non-clumping mist when atomized, and acts as an effective natural preservative against bacterial contamination.
